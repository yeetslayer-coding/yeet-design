# B03 — Fünsu: ürün maskotu ve eylem varyasyonları

[Kaynak görsel — sağ üst bölüm](mascot-systems-source.jpg) · [Dizin](index.md)

## Görsel çözümleme

Kırmızı fonda krem/sarı, hafif kırık kenarlı yatay isim plakası; yumuşak, elde çizilmiş gibi duran kırmızı harfler ve altında krem kategori adı. Turuncu tatlı patates maskotu mavi tulum ve hasır şapka giyiyor; üstte yeşil yapraklar, yanaklarda pembe vurgular var. Ana pozda ürün yiyor, başka pozda scooter sürüyor veya ayakta duruyor. Alttaki küçük sarı/mavi/kırmızı dikdörtgen dizisi çanta, kart ve yatay afişlere bağlanan bir kenar sistemi. Kaynakta Tayca yazılar mevcut; okunamayan küçük kopyalar ve kuruluş/ürün iddiaları türetilmez.

## Üretim prompt’u

```text
Create an original mascot-led identity board for [BRAND], a [CATEGORY] food brand. Portrait Instagram format, 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5. Build the palette from strong warm red, pale butter yellow, sky blue, orange and tiny green accents. Lead with a large warm-red identity panel: an original anthropomorphic category-ingredient character at left and a hand-shaped pale-yellow wordmark plaque at right. The lettering is rounded, irregular and friendly, with a simple cream category descriptor beneath. The character has a clear recognizable body silhouette, a straw-style hat, small botanical detail and blue workwear; invent a distinctive face and construction for this brand rather than cloning the reference potato.

Show the same character in three coherent actions: tasting the product, riding a small delivery scooter, and standing in a welcoming pose. Keep eyes, cheeks, limbs, hat, clothing and stroke weight identical as a model system. Use these poses selectively across a paper shopping bag, compact label, die-cut sticker, square campaign card and horizontal shop banner. Add one restrained repeating edge motif made from alternating small red, yellow and blue rectangular blocks; reuse the same spacing and proportions across applications. Let the logo plaque and character work both together and independently.

Keep the drawings flat with controlled linework, sparse shadows and readable negative spaces. The bag may show believable paper folds, but do not turn every graphic into 3D. Use [LOGO_MASTER] and [APPROVED_COPY] consistently. Include multiple languages only when exact approved strings are supplied and can be checked; do not imitate unknown script with gibberish. Omit source credits, dates, contact details, prices and product claims. Make the main identity and secondary applications readable at 4:5 rather than squeezing a long presentation into tiny panels.
```
