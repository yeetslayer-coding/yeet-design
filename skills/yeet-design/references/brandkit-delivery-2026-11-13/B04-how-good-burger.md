# B04 — How Good Burger: çift maskot ve farklı logo düzenleri

[Kaynak görsel — sol alt bölüm](mascot-systems-source.jpg) · [Dizin](index.md)

## Görsel çözümleme

Kırmızı ana panelde üstte iri krem serif başlık; önde iki köpek başı: biri siyah/beyaz ve aşçı şapkalı, diğeri açık kahve ve büyük kulaklı. Krem dış kontur, kalın koyu çizgi, pembe iç kulak; mavi rozet sınırlı kontrast oluşturuyor. Kırmızı/krem dama şeridi, yıldızlar ve alt bilgi bandı hiyerarşiyi bağlıyor. Alt uygulamada sarı akışkan script marka yazısı, her köpek için ayrı yuvarlak mühür, uzun banner, çanta, dikey kart ve telefon kılıfı var. Serif başlık ve script logo farklı görevlerde; hepsi aynı font sanılmamalı. “100% BEEF”, yıl, üstünlük sloganı ve yabancı yazılar kopyalanacak ürün verisi değildir.

## Üretim prompt’u

```text
Design a coherent original burger-brand identity presentation for [BRAND] on a portrait 4:5 canvas, target 1080 × 1350 px. Use red and ivory as the main fields, mustard yellow for a lively script wordmark, small blue accents and restrained pink within character details. Develop two complementary original dog mascots: one with dark-and-light patches and a chef's hat, the other with a warm tan face and a distinct large-ear silhouette. Keep thick dark interior strokes, cream die-cut borders, expressive eyes and consistent proportions. Do not copy the source dogs.

In the main identity panel, place a broad cream serif [APPROVED_COPY] headline above the paired character heads. Use a short red-and-cream checker band and a small clean star accent to anchor the lower edge. Below, show the actual brand wordmark in a custom flowing mustard script, then two individual circular mascot seals that share the same ring proportions and type system. Continue into a slim horizontal banner, carrier bag, vertical printed card and phone-case application. Each object should use a purposeful combination of wordmark, one mascot, paired mascots or checker border; vary composition without changing the identity.

Preserve a single [LOGO_MASTER], repeatable character construction and consistent border rhythm. Distinguish the serif headline role from the script brand lettering; do not substitute unrelated fonts on every item. Keep illustrations flat and mockup materials credible, with short appropriate shadows where physical objects appear. Use only verified supplied copy. Omit source percentages, dates, superiority claims, unfamiliar script and designer credits. Keep the board readable; reduce small secondary pieces or use an approved extra page instead of producing illegible microtext.
```
